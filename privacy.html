Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

Growing in Grace provides free Christian discipleship teaching and tools for tracking curriculum progress. This policy explains what information is collected when you create or use an account.

Information We Collect

We collect the name and email address associated with your account. If you sign in with Google, Google provides basic account identity information such as your name and email address. We also store your lesson activity, completion status, and most recently visited lesson.

How We Use Information

We use this information to authenticate your account, personalize your dashboard, save lesson progress, provide account support, and maintain the security and reliability of the curriculum platform. We do not sell your personal information.

Service Providers

Account authentication and curriculum progress are processed using Supabase. Google processes information when you choose Google sign-in. The website is hosted through Netlify. These providers may process limited information as necessary to deliver their services.

Data Retention and Your Choices

Account and lesson information is retained while your account is active. You may stop using Google sign-in, revoke Growing in Grace access through your Google Account, or request deletion of your Growing in Grace account and associated progress information.

Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards and established service providers to protect account information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, email andrewramirezmbc@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the site or its services change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are made.
